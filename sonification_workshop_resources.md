**`Guiding Questions:`** 

* `What aspects of sound can we/do we act to control?`  
* `What types of information are conveyed in sound? In what contexts?`  
* `What choices do you find yourself making about your own personal soundscape?`  
* `What are some of the ways listening differs from reading/viewing? What are the ways they are alike?`
